# Method notes shared by every synthetic case

These notes apply to all the synthetic cases. They are kept here once instead of repeated in each case.

## Notes

- An authorized operator accepts a relevant request or scheduled obligation into the shared workflow.
- Approve a bounded live pilot only after requirements and exceptions have named owners.
- Choose the least complex intervention addressing the supported handoff failure; compare added administration and integration burden.
- Collect at least one representative operating cycle before rollout; for monthly billing or maintenance include the relevant full cycle.
- Do not infer staff reductions from reclaimed task minutes.
- Do not replace the whole service platform or deploy autonomous agents solely because the owner reports inefficiency.
- Extend sampling when volumes or seasonality make the sample unrepresentative.
- If discriminating evidence is absent or conflicting, return an unresolved hypothesis plus a targeted question, not a confident solution.
- Include administrative overhead and rework.
- No fixed performance threshold is claimed without customer agreement.
- No price assigned; current rate card must not be extended by analogy.
- Pause automated writes and notifications, retain the event log, export outstanding work and return to a named manual coordinator.
- Process owner reviews coverage of acceptance cases, unresolved critical failures, metric direction and staff workload.
- Qualitative case-world support only; requires fresh customer evidence before reuse.
- Reconcile identities and existing obligations with the process owner.
- Reconcile queued events before resuming to avoid duplicate actions.
- Record missingness and changed case mix.
- Review outcome metrics, added administrative work and failure logs before expanding.
- Revise the causal hypothesis and request the corresponding source record; do not reuse this fictional answer for a new customer.
- Routine coordination became explicit; unresolved exceptions continued to require a person.
- Run the proposed rules in shadow mode; record disagreements and missed exceptions.
- Start with one consenting customer group and the staff who own its handoffs; include a normal case and each listed exception.
- The team adopted the proposed workflow for the pilot scope.
- Use a stable denominator and comparable operating periods.
- User-provided fictional owner stories in this conversation; opening narrative below is a faithful condensed adaptation, not a verbatim transcript.
- Verify customer-specific permissions, identities, rules and system integration support.
