# Evidence and synthetic cases

## Synthetic cases are analogies

Every case article in this knowledge base is synthetic, including the playbook and blueprint material it holds: an invented business, written for learning. A case can show a pattern that may apply, a question worth asking or an intervention worth considering. It is never evidence about the owner's business. A synthetic case may describe an observation as confirmed, but that confirmation belongs to the fictional business only.

When the assistant cites a case id such as C05, it is pointing to analogous experience, not to a fact about the owner. The assistant never infers the owner's headcount, software, permissions, contract clauses, channel support or savings from a retrieved case. Where a fact is missing, the right result is an open question or a proposal that states its condition, not a borrowed number.

The same symptom does not imply the same cause. Two businesses that both complain about late visits may have very different problems, one with site access, another with stock. The assistant compares how closely a case matches before relying on it, and looks for at least one plausible alternative explanation.

## Customer facts keep their source and status

A fact the owner states during the diagnosis is recorded together with the owner's own words, the time it was captured and its status. The status says whether the fact is reported, verified, conflicting or unknown. What the owner says is reported until a record or a person confirms it; repeating a statement does not verify it, and neither does a retrieved passage that sounds similar.

Numbers deserve particular care. An owner who says "about 50 calls a week" has given an approximate count of calls, which does not establish 50 unique incidents. The assistant records the figure with its units and period as stated.

## Conflicting values are kept, not averaged

When the owner gives two different values for the same fact, for example eight technicians early in the conversation and eleven later, both values are kept and the fact is marked as conflicting. The assistant does not average them and does not silently pick one. It asks the owner which value is right, or whether both are true in different senses, such as eight full-time and three part-time.

## Lifecycle

Synthetic content can be corrected or replaced, but its synthetic origin never changes. Each article keeps a stable id, and a substantive revision changes its date.
