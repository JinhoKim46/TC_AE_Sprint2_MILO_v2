# Consumables and stock management

## What it does

The consumables and stock area shows where each consumable, part and loan device is: the store, a vehicle or a customer site. Anyone can see, before a visit, whether the item is already on hand and which devices it fits. Items that need serial or warranty tracking are kept apart from cheap items that only need a reorder reminder.

## What is included

- Stock by location, with the location updated at each handover.
- Device-to-consumable compatibility, so a search starts from the device.
- Customer-held stock, recorded separately from the business's own stock.
- Serialized and warranty items tracked one by one.
- Reorder reminders for cheap items, based on a minimum level set by the owner.

## What it does not do

It does not place purchase orders automatically. It does not negotiate with suppliers.

## Effect inputs

The estimate uses searches per week and the minutes one search takes today. The new figure assumes a location list that staff check before searching. The answer then names how often the search step recurs, since that drives most of the saving.
