# Contract management

## What it does

The Contract management module keeps each customer's contract terms in one dated record: which devices are covered, how many preventive visits are owed, who may approve extra work at each site and when the agreement renews. Before a technician is sent, it flags work that may fall outside coverage, so the question of who pays is asked before the repair rather than after the invoice is disputed.

## What is included

- A coverage check before dispatch that compares the device and the fault with the covered items in the contract.
- Preventive-visit checklists that define what a completed preventive visit must contain.
- Approval routing by site, for customers whose branches and head office have different approval contacts.
- Renewal reminders with account history, so the person preparing a renewal sees recent visits and complaints.
- Workload per contract, showing how much service time each agreement actually consumes.

## What it does not do

The contract-management module does not make automatic renewal offers; a person decides what to propose. The contract-management module does not perform legal contract review, and unclear clauses go to someone qualified to read them. The contract-management module does not present profit figures as exact, because the cost of service time is an estimate.

## Connecting it to your tools

- Spreadsheet import: contracts held in spreadsheets reach contract management as CSV files, and changes go back the same way; no live connection is made. It adds nothing to the monthly price.
- Accounting export: for contract management, the charges it prepares are written to a file that the accounts team imports into the accounting software. It adds 100 USD a month.

An integration is charged once however many modules share it, so contract management adds no second accounting export fee when meter billing already uses that export.

## Price

The base monthly price for contract-management is 480 USD. Contract management also has a one-time setup fee of 1200 USD. The 480 USD base is multiplied by the technician factor: 1.00 for a field team of up to 5 technicians, 1.30 for up to 15 and 1.60 for up to 30.
