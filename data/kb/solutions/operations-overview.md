# Operations overview

## What it does

The operations overview gives the owner one board of open work. Each item has an owner, a next action and, if it is stuck, the reason it is waiting: parts, approval, the customer or a supplier. Commitments made to customers are written down on the same board, so they do not depend on one person's memory.

## What is included

- An open work board, filtered by owner, site and waiting state.
- An owner and a next action for every item.
- Blocker states: waiting for parts, waiting for approval, waiting for the customer, waiting for a supplier.
- A commitment log with the promise, the person who made it and the date.
- Reviewed fix notes, so a solved problem can be found again.

## What it does not do

It does not close work without a person. It does not report figures that staff have not entered. It does not diagnose faults.

## Effect inputs

The estimate uses status questions per week and the minutes it takes to answer one today. The new figure assumes the board answers most questions directly. The answer then names how many open items the board would need to show at once.
