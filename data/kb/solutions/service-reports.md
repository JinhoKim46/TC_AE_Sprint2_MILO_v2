# Service reports

## What it does

The Service reports module captures the evidence of finished work while the technician is still on site: which device, what fault, what was done, which parts were used and whether the customer confirmed it. The same capture works for the business's own staff and for subcontractors. From that evidence it prepares the report each customer expects, so finished jobs stop waiting for paperwork at the end of the week.

## What is included

- On-site completion capture with a short checklist matched to the evidence the customer requires.
- Report templates per customer, because a school, a clinic and a law office rarely want the same form.
- A pending-confirmation status for work that is technically complete but not yet accepted by the customer.
- Subcontractor job instructions and spending limits, so outside technicians know the scope and the amount they may spend without asking.

## What it does not do

The service-reports module does not record a confirmation that was not given; an absent contact leaves the job pending. The service-reports module does not submit reports to customer portals without the customer giving access to them.

## Connecting it to your tools

- Email capture: service reports can pick up confirmations and replies from a shared mailbox, read with the business's permission, after one working day of setup. It adds 150 USD a month.
- Service software API: when the service management application offers an API with an access key, service reports can read job details from it; that connection takes two working days. For service reports this connection costs 300 USD a month.

For service reports, a mailbox or API that another chosen module already connects to is not charged again; each integration surcharge counts once for the whole estimate.

## Price

The base monthly price for service-reports is 340 USD. On top of that, service reports has a one-time setup fee of 1200 USD. Its base price follows the technician factor, 1.00 for up to 5 field technicians, 1.30 for up to 15 and 1.60 for up to 30, because each technician produces reports.
