# C12: Senior technicians are the only knowledge base

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

13 staff; two long-serving technicians carry most customer-specific knowledge.

“Juniors call seniors while both are on jobs. Manuals and old notes are scattered across folders and chat. A recurring school printer configuration is repeatedly rediscovered. End-of-day notes just say fixed, and seniors worry about procedures being applied without their exceptions.”

**Typical symptom:** Juniors call seniors while both are on jobs. End-of-day notes just say fixed, and seniors worry about procedures being applied without their exceptions.

## Current workflow

1. Junior receives brief symptoms
2. Attempts diagnosis
3. Calls senior
4. Senior interrupts own work
5. Fix applied
6. Minimal note saved

## Diagnostic questions

### C12-Q01: Is the fix general or site-specific?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** It depends on the school’s print-server configuration.

**Why ask:** Generalizing can be harmful.

**If the answer differs:** If the problem requires specialist judgement or physical experience, documents may reduce interruptions but cannot replace training.

### C12-Q02: Do notes describe applicability?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** They rarely include software version or prerequisites.

**Why ask:** Retrieval needs conditions.

### C12-Q03: Who approves knowledge entries?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Nobody currently owns review.

**Why ask:** Unreviewed notes cannot become authoritative procedures.

### C12-Q04: Can capture happen during work?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Short dictated notes are feasible; long forms are not.

**Why ask:** Reduce capture burden.

## Diagnosis

**Supported in this case:** Resolution context is not captured, scoped, reviewed or retired, so experience cannot transfer reliably.

## Minimum evidence before reusing this pattern

- A simulated ticket sequence repeats the same configuration issue.
- A simulated folder contains obsolete instructions.
- A simulated senior review identifies a missing version prerequisite.

## Interventions

**Process first:** Capture symptoms, environment, tests, result and exceptions; assign review owners and retirement dates.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** Draft knowledge articles from redacted job notes; retrieve by environment and scope; show sources and review status to technicians.

## Data fields

`article_id`, `customer_scope`, `asset_family`, `software_version`, `prerequisites`, `diagnostic_steps`, `verified_result`, `author`, `reviewer`, `reviewed_at`, `superseded_by`

## Business rules

- C12-R01: Unreviewed articles are suggestions, not approved instructions.
- C12-R02: Customer-specific content is access scoped.
- C12-R03: No credentials in indexed notes.
- C12-R04: Retired articles cannot appear as current procedures.

## Acceptance tests

- C12-AT01: A version-mismatched procedure carries a warning.
- C12-AT02: A junior cannot retrieve another customer’s restricted configuration.
- C12-AT03: A superseded article links to its replacement.

## Human review

Actual solution accuracy requires expert review; synthetic procedures must never become device repair authority.

## How to measure

**Primary metric:** Repeat investigations requiring senior escalation / eligible repeat incidents; also monitor incorrect procedure application.
