# C16: Clinic support must respect operational windows

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

Seven staff supporting clinic office IT, printers, networks and backups; clinical devices excluded.

“Reception problems are urgent when patients are waiting, but we cannot restart systems whenever we like. Vendors handle clinical software and devices. An intermittent problem disappeared during a short visit and returned next morning. We need useful notes without patient details.”

**Typical symptom:** Reception problems are urgent when patients are waiting, but we cannot restart systems whenever we like. We need useful notes without patient details.

## Current workflow

1. Clinic reports disruption
2. Determine affected service
3. Seek authorization
4. Attempt diagnosis within window
5. Escalate vendor or schedule work
6. Confirm restoration

## Diagnostic questions

### C16-Q01: Which service is in our scope?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Reception PCs and network, not clinical equipment.

**Why ask:** Avoid unsupported responsibility.

**If the answer differs:** A vendor outage or clinical-device failure needs routing to the responsible supplier, not an invented IT repair procedure.

### C16-Q02: Who authorizes interruption?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** The practice manager coordinates acceptable downtime.

**Why ask:** Work timing is an approval constraint.

### C16-Q03: What evidence reproduces the fault?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Time, workstation and error code help; patient screenshots are unnecessary.

**Why ask:** Minimize sensitive collection.

### C16-Q04: Was disappearance treated as resolution?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Yes, although no cause was found.

**Why ask:** Distinguish unable-to-reproduce from resolved.

## Diagnosis

## Interventions

**Process first:** Record operational impact using non-patient information; identify the authorized coordinator and approved work window; retain monitoring follow-up.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** Guide privacy-minimizing intake, scope routing and authorization tracking; draft updates without clinical details.

## Business rules

- C16-R01: Do not collect patient records for routine IT triage.
- C16-R02: Disruptive changes require current scope-specific approval.
- C16-R03: Not reproduced remains distinct from confirmed resolution.
- C16-R04: Clinical advice and clinical-device procedures stay outside this case.

## Acceptance tests

- C16-AT01: A patient-bearing screenshot is flagged for controlled handling, not indexed.
- C16-AT02: No restart is scheduled outside the approved window.
- C16-AT03: A recurring symptom reopens investigation with prior evidence.
