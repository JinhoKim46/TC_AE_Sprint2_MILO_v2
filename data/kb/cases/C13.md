# C13: Completed work waits for paperwork

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

16 staff; institutional customers require service documents before invoicing.

“Technicians use paper or photos, and office staff retype reports for each customer. Signatures or device identifiers are missing. Chargeable jobs wait for invoices even though repairs are complete. Sometimes the contact is absent when work finishes.”

**Typical symptom:** Technicians use paper or photos, and office staff retype reports for each customer. Sometimes the contact is absent when work finishes.

## Current workflow

1. Repair completed
2. Paper notes captured
3. Office transcribes
4. Missing information chased
5. Customer report prepared
6. Acceptance obtained
7. Invoice released

## Diagnostic questions

### C13-Q01: Which fields actually block invoicing?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Device, work description and acceptance evidence vary by customer.

**Why ask:** Use customer-specific requirements.

**If the answer differs:** If complete reports are waiting on a customer’s payment dispute, automating documents does not resolve the commercial issue.

### C13-Q02: When are omissions discovered?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** During office transcription, often the next day.

**Why ask:** Validate while context is fresh.

### C13-Q03: What does a signature mean?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Usually acceptance of the described work, not merely attendance.

**Why ask:** Preserve acceptance semantics.

### C13-Q04: What if the contact is absent?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Technicians leave a blank signature and the office chases it.

**Why ask:** A legitimate pending state is needed.

## Interventions

**Process first:** Use a short on-site checklist aligned to required evidence; represent completed work and accepted work separately.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** Capture structured notes and evidence once; render reviewed customer templates; queue missing acceptance for follow-up.

- C13-R01: Never synthesize a signature or imply acceptance from an absent contact.
