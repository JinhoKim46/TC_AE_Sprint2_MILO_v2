# C10: Subcontractor work lacks acceptance evidence

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

Eight internal staff plus regional external service partners.

“We send partners addresses and symptoms by message. Some return detailed reports; others only say done. One replaced a part with branch permission, but head office refused to pay. Assignment time was mistaken for an agreed arrival time during another complaint.”

**Typical symptom:** We send partners addresses and symptoms by message. Assignment time was mistaken for an agreed arrival time during another complaint.

## Current workflow

1. Coordinator sends job
2. Partner accepts
3. Appointment agreed
4. Work performed
5. Short message returned
6. Customer report and partner payment

## Diagnostic questions

### C10-Q01: Who may approve additional cost?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Head office, not the branch contact.

**Why ask:** Separate site access from spending authority.

**If the answer differs:** If detailed instructions were acknowledged and repeatedly ignored, partner performance management may be needed beyond software.

### C10-Q02: Do we record acceptance and appointment times?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Only the outbound message time.

**Why ask:** Dispatch is not a commitment.

### C10-Q03: What evidence is mandatory?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Requirements are not included consistently.

**Why ask:** Standardize instructions before measuring compliance.

### C10-Q04: Will partners install our application?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Most will use a simple phone page but resist another account workflow.

**Why ask:** Adoption constrains interface design.

## Evidence in the fictional case

- C10-E01: A simulated partner invoice includes an unapproved replacement.
- C10-E02: A simulated assignment contains no spending limit.
- C10-E03: A simulated report says only done.

## Diagnosis

**Supported in this case:** Delegated work lacks explicit scope, authority, milestones and a completion contract.

## Minimum evidence before reusing this pattern

- A simulated partner invoice includes an unapproved replacement.
- A simulated assignment contains no spending limit.
- A simulated report says only done.

## Interventions

**Process first:** Send a concise work order with scope, escalation contact and evidence requirements; separate technical completion from customer acceptance.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** Provide authenticated, job-scoped mobile reporting; record acceptance, agreed window, actual arrival, parts and approval requests.

## Data fields

`work_order_id`, `partner_id`, `permitted_site`, `scope`, `spending_limit`, `accepted_at`, `appointment_window`, `arrived_at`, `completion_evidence`, `customer_acceptance`

## Business rules

- C10-R01: Partners can access only assigned jobs.
- C10-R02: Extra spending requires the authorized customer approver.
- C10-R03: Done is not accepted when mandatory evidence is missing.
- C10-R04: Expired access links cannot reveal job data.

## Acceptance tests

- C10-AT01: A branch employee cannot approve head-office-only expenditure.
- C10-AT02: One partner cannot view another partner’s jobs.
- C10-AT03: Missing evidence creates follow-up rather than fabricated completion.

## Human review

Partner access, payout terms and required evidence must be agreed contractually.

## How to measure

**Primary metric:** Partner jobs accepted without documentation rework / completed partner jobs; track unauthorized spend and missed agreed windows.
