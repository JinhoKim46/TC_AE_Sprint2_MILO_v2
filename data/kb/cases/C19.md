# C19: Service software increases duplicate work

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

19 staff; service management software introduced eight months ago.

“The office creates tickets then messages technicians because chat gets noticed. Updates return by chat and are copied back. Long mobile forms and poor connectivity push reporting into the evening. Management asks for separate spreadsheet totals because the dashboard is incomplete.”

**Typical symptom:** The office creates tickets then messages technicians because chat gets noticed. Management asks for separate spreadsheet totals because the dashboard is incomplete.

## Current workflow

1. Office opens ticket
2. Office duplicates assignment in chat
3. Technician works
4. Update sent in chat
5. Office rekeys
6. Manager asks for spreadsheet

## Diagnostic questions

### C19-Q01: Which fields prevent completion?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Several mandatory fields do not apply to simple jobs.

**Why ask:** Workflow configuration may be the obstacle.

**If the answer differs:** If essential features genuinely cannot be configured or integrated, replacement may be justified after a bounded trial; do not assume another app is always wrong.

### C19-Q02: Can staff save offline drafts?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** No reliable path exists at some sites.

**Why ask:** Connectivity is a concrete constraint.

### C19-Q03: Why does management use spreadsheet?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Dashboard totals omit late entries.

**Why ask:** Trust and timing form a feedback loop.

### C19-Q04: Does one staff member’s apparent productivity depend on reporting detail?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Yes; careful entries record more time.

**Why ask:** Avoid unfair comparisons from incomplete data.

## Interventions

**Process first:** Observe actual work; remove unjustified fields; define one authoritative queue and reconcile exceptions before retiring the shadow sheet.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** First configure existing software; add reliable notifications and offline draft synchronization only where supported and needed.

## Business rules

- C19-R01: Offline draft is not server-confirmed completion.
- C19-R02: Resolve conflicting edits visibly.
- C19-R03: Do not judge employee performance from incomparable recording completeness.
- C19-R04: Preserve a documented fallback when synchronization fails.

## Acceptance tests

- C19-AT01: A minimal simple-job form can be submitted without irrelevant data.
- C19-AT02: Reconnection does not create duplicate jobs.
- C19-AT03: Conflicting edits appear for resolution rather than overwriting silently.

## Human review

Existing software configuration and API/offline support must be tested before proposing custom development.

**Primary metric:** Duplicate-entry minutes per job and reporting completeness; track sync failures and staff adoption.
