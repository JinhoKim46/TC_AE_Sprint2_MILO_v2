# C05: Meter readings delay and corrupt invoices

> Synthetic teaching case: invented for learning, not a customer record. Example answers and evidence belong to this fictional business only; ask the owner rather than assume they apply.

## Business and opening narrative

Managed print provider; approximately 420 devices with usage-based agreements.

“Our administrator collects meter photos, remote readings and technician notes. Contracts have different color and monochrome allowances; some pool machines. A replacement device’s opening reading was attached to the old machine, producing a disputed bill.”

**Typical symptom:** Our administrator collects meter photos, remote readings and technician notes. A replacement device’s opening reading was attached to the old machine, producing a disputed bill.

## Current workflow

1. Collect reading
2. Match machine
3. Enter counters
4. Apply contract terms
5. Review exceptions
6. Send billing data

## Diagnostic questions

### C05-Q01: Can counters be linked to device identity?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Photos often omit the serial, and filenames identify only the customer.

**Why ask:** Device linkage is necessary before arithmetic.

**If the answer differs:** If identity and readings are correct but a tariff formula is wrong, repair the calculation; do not demand additional photographs.

### C05-Q02: What happens at replacement?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** We do not consistently capture the old closing and new opening readings.

**Why ask:** Billing needs separate device segments.

### C05-Q03: Are special allowances structured?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** Some exist only in emails and the administrator’s memory.

**Why ask:** Correct extraction cannot fix missing commercial rules.

### C05-Q04: Can a counter decrease legitimately?

**Example answer in this fictional case:** A replacement or reset can cause it, but ordinary readings should not.

**Why ask:** Detect exceptions rather than forcing a plausible value.

## Evidence in the fictional case

- C05-E01: A simulated replacement worksheet lacks a closing reading.
- C05-E02: A simulated photo links to the wrong serial.
- C05-E03: A simulated agreement contains a pooled allowance.

## Diagnosis

**Supported in this case:** Reading provenance and effective-dated device/contract mapping are missing from billing preparation.

## Minimum evidence before reusing this pattern

- A simulated replacement worksheet lacks a closing reading.
- A simulated photo links to the wrong serial.
- A simulated agreement contains a pooled allowance.

## Interventions

**Process first:** Normalize reviewed contract terms; record installation and removal readings; hold unresolved segments for accounts review.

**Automation and bounded AI help:** Extract reading drafts with image references; validate chronology and counters; calculate usage with deterministic decimal rules and produce an exception queue.

## Data fields

`device_id`, `reading_id`, `capture_time`, `mono_counter`, `color_counter`, `source_hash`, `install_reading`, `removal_reading`, `tariff_version`, `allowance_pool`, `billing_period`

## Business rules

- C05-R01: Negative deltas block ordinary billing unless an approved reset or replacement segment explains them.
- C05-R02: Pool allowance is applied once per defined group and period.
- C05-R03: OCR confidence never authorizes invoice sending.
- C05-R04: Corrected readings retain the original and reason.

## Acceptance tests

- C05-AT01: Old and new devices are calculated as distinct segments.
- C05-AT02: A repeated image hash raises a duplicate warning.
- C05-AT03: A pooled allowance is not subtracted once per device.

## Human review

Proration, taxes, minimum charges, resets and rounding rules must come from the customer’s approved terms.

## How to measure

**Primary metric:** Invoices requiring correction / invoices issued; separately measure days from period close to review-ready billing.
