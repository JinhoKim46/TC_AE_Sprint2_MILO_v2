# C18 — Warehouse, van and customer stock diverge

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 18; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

24 staff; 16 field technicians, central warehouse and customer-held loan equipment.

“Purchases, warehouse counts and van notes never agree. We buy parts that another technician already has. Faulty items return without job references, losing warranty credits. Cheap cables should not require the same administration as expensive serialized components.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Purchase
2. Receive
3. Transfer to van
4. Install or loan
5. Return faulty item
6. Seek credit or scrap
7. Reconcile sporadically

The critical failure is: Inventory lacks a consistent movement ledger, disposition and serialized-loan lifecycle.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Segment stock by tracking needs; log high-value transfers and returns; quarantine faulty items and assign return owners.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Use barcode-assisted movements, location balances, loan obligations and warranty deadline tracking.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.
