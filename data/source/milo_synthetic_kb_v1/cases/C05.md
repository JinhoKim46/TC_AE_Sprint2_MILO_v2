# C05 — Meter readings delay and corrupt invoices

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 5; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Managed print provider; approximately 420 devices with usage-based agreements.

“Our administrator collects meter photos, remote readings and technician notes. Contracts have different color and monochrome allowances; some pool machines. A replacement device’s opening reading was attached to the old machine, producing a disputed bill.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Collect reading
2. Match machine
3. Enter counters
4. Apply contract terms
5. Review exceptions
6. Send billing data

The critical failure is: Reading provenance and effective-dated device/contract mapping are missing from billing preparation.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Normalize reviewed contract terms; record installation and removal readings; hold unresolved segments for accounts review.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Extract reading drafts with image references; validate chronology and counters; calculate usage with deterministic decimal rules and produce an exception queue.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
