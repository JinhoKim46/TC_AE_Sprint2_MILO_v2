# C10 — Subcontractor work lacks acceptance evidence

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 10; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Eight internal staff plus regional external service partners.

“We send partners addresses and symptoms by message. Some return detailed reports; others only say done. One replaced a part with branch permission, but head office refused to pay. Assignment time was mistaken for an agreed arrival time during another complaint.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Coordinator sends job
2. Partner accepts
3. Appointment agreed
4. Work performed
5. Short message returned
6. Customer report and partner payment

The critical failure is: Delegated work lacks explicit scope, authority, milestones and a completion contract.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Send a concise work order with scope, escalation contact and evidence requirements; separate technical completion from customer acceptance.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Provide authenticated, job-scoped mobile reporting; record acceptance, agreed window, actual arrival, parts and approval requests.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
