# C04 — Toner stock exists but cannot be found

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 4; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Printer rental and consumables provider; approximately 650 managed devices.

“The spreadsheet says five cartridges, but some are in vans, reserved or already delivered. Customers identify a printer by its location and sometimes photograph the wrong one. Leaving spare toner helped some sites but caused others to stockpile it.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Customer reports toner warning
2. Office identifies device
3. Spreadsheet checked
4. Cartridge picked
5. Delivery made
6. Stock updated later

The critical failure is: Missing location and reservation accounting combines with unreliable device and customer-stock identification.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Count high-use stock; assign device IDs; log transfers at handover; ask about usable on-site spare stock before urgent delivery.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Maintain a location ledger and compatibility table; reserve stock atomically; suggest replenishment for human approval until counts are reliable.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
