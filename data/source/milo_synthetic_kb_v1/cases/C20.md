# C20 — Owner memory is the operating system

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 20; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Six staff: owner, four technicians and administrator; plans to hire two more technicians.

“I coordinate everything through messages and memory. When I took three days away, someone revisited a customer before the part arrived, and invoices stopped because nobody knew what I promised. We have spreadsheets and calendars, but I connect them in my head.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Owner reviews messages
2. Owner directs staff
3. Staff ask owner for decisions
4. Owner resolves exceptions
5. Owner tells office what to bill

The critical failure is: Commitments, dependencies and delegated authority are person-bound rather than recorded in a shared workflow.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Create one lightweight commitment board with owner, next action and blocker; publish agreed routine decision boundaries.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Capture message-derived task drafts for confirmation; remind responsible staff about dependencies; escalate exceptions beyond agreed authority.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
