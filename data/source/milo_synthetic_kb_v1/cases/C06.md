# C06 — Contract coverage discovered after repair

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 6; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

18 staff; approximately 110 mixed PC, printer and network maintenance customers.

“We dispatch when the customer is under contract, but that does not mean the device or work is covered. A personal laptop was repaired at an office, and a part replacement was waived because nobody warned the customer about the charge. Lists are outdated.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Customer calls
2. Company contract checked
3. Technician dispatched
4. Work performed
5. Office checks chargeability
6. Dispute or invoice

The critical failure is: Coverage decisions occur after irreversible work because scope and approval authority are not available at the point of service.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Define covered, excluded and uncertain states; provide a pre-work approval path and documented goodwill authority.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Retrieve the effective agreement and asset scope; surface coverage evidence; create approval requests when scope changes.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
