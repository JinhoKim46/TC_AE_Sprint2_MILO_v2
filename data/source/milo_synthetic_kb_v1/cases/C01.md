# C01 — Duplicate dispatch across contact channels

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 1; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

14 staff; nine field technicians; approximately 90 office and school customers; monthly maintenance with some parts extra.

“Teachers contact our office, their usual technician, or me. Our office uses Excel and group chat. Two technicians attended the same printer after different teachers described the problem differently. Other requests disappear because everybody assumes somebody else accepted them. Customers will keep calling, so a mandatory customer portal will not solve this alone.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Customer reports by phone or message
2. Recipient records selectively
3. Coordinator assigns in chat
4. Technician travels
5. Repair reported informally

The critical failure is: Requests and commitments bypass the shared queue; asset ambiguity makes the bypass harder to detect.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Nominate the coordinator as assignment owner; require a short direct-contact capture before promising attendance, allowing an urgent exception with later reconciliation.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Create intake drafts from supported channels; show related open incidents and active assignments; require a coordinator decision on ambiguous duplicates.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
