# C08 — Remote-first support creates another delay

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 8; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

12 staff supporting approximately 75 small businesses.

“We visit for simple settings problems, but attempted remote support can also waste 25 minutes before a visit. The remote tool may be absent, the internet down or the caller unauthorized. Customers repeat symptoms to several staff members.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Call received
2. Ask for visit
3. Optional remote attempt
4. Connection fails or diagnosis proceeds
5. Dispatch
6. Customer repeats story

The critical failure is: There is no eligibility gate or structured handover for remote support.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Ask connectivity, authorization and symptom questions first; agree a limited remote attempt and a clear escalation point.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Apply a deterministic eligibility checklist; suggest remote or site triage; retain tests, errors and consent references.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
