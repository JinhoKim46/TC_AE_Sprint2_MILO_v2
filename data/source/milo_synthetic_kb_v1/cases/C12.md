# C12 — Senior technicians are the only knowledge base

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 12; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

13 staff; two long-serving technicians carry most customer-specific knowledge.

“Juniors call seniors while both are on jobs. Manuals and old notes are scattered across folders and chat. A recurring school printer configuration is repeatedly rediscovered. End-of-day notes just say fixed, and seniors worry about procedures being applied without their exceptions.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Junior receives brief symptoms
2. Attempts diagnosis
3. Calls senior
4. Senior interrupts own work
5. Fix applied
6. Minimal note saved

The critical failure is: Resolution context is not captured, scoped, reviewed or retired, so experience cannot transfer reliably.

2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Draft knowledge articles from redacted job notes; retrieve by environment and scope; show sources and review status to technicians.
