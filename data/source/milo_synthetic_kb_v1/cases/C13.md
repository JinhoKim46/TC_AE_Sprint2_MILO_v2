# C13 — Completed work waits for paperwork

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 13; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

16 staff; institutional customers require service documents before invoicing.

“Technicians use paper or photos, and office staff retype reports for each customer. Signatures or device identifiers are missing. Chargeable jobs wait for invoices even though repairs are complete. Sometimes the contact is absent when work finishes.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Repair completed
2. Paper notes captured
3. Office transcribes
4. Missing information chased
5. Customer report prepared
6. Acceptance obtained
7. Invoice released

The critical failure is: Evidence capture is disconnected from customer document requirements and acceptance workflow.
