# C17 — Vendor escalation becomes a ticket graveyard

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 17; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Ten-person office network, Wi-Fi and general IT service provider.

“The internet provider wants wired tests; the application vendor wants logs. Our notes rarely say where or when tests occurred. Second technicians repeat them. An access point replacement did not fix a meeting-room problem that returned during busy events. Waiting for vendor can last indefinitely.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Customer reports slowness
2. Technician tests locally
3. Suspected supplier contacted
4. More evidence requested
5. Customer waits
6. Workaround applied
7. Ticket closed or forgotten

The critical failure is: Evidence lacks reproducible context and escalations lose accountable ownership.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Record test context, results and next vendor action; separate restored, monitoring and resolved states.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Build structured investigation timelines and vendor follow-up tasks; draft evidence packages with sensitive information removed.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
