# C03 — Preventive maintenance without defined completion

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 3; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

11 staff; 38 school contracts with regular visits and reactive support.

“A visit usually means asking whether anything is broken and getting a signature. Different technicians check different things. We sometimes return for scheduled maintenance days after an emergency visit, yet a signed sheet can hide an inaccessible computer room.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Administrator schedules school
2. Technician attends
3. Available equipment checked
4. Contact signs
5. Visit marked complete

The critical failure is: The system tracks attendance instead of contract-level maintenance obligations and partial completion.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Translate reviewed contracts into task lists; distinguish attended, partially completed and obligation fulfilled.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Track due tasks per site and asset; suggest combining eligible checks with reactive visits; carry incomplete work forward.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
