# C07 — Repairs stall between diagnosis and parts arrival

> SYNTHETIC CASE. Author-generated, not domain-validated. No real customer data, verified savings or deployed results. Narrative adapted from supplied story 7; interview answers and evidence are invented teaching fixtures.

## Business and opening narrative

Nine-person business PC and laptop repair provider.

“Technicians identify a part, office staff ask suppliers, and customers approve quotes. The gaps between those steps last days. An approval sent to a technician never reached purchasing. Shelves and spreadsheets disagree, and warranty return dates get missed.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

1. Diagnose
2. Identify exact part
3. Request supplier quote
4. Seek customer approval
5. Order
6. Receive
7. Repair
8. Return loaner and close

The critical failure is: Handoffs lack an accountable next action, complete part identity and explicit deadlines.

## Diagnosis and competing explanation

**Supported only within this fictional case:** Handoffs lack an accountable next action, complete part identity and explicit deadlines.

**Alternative / non-applicability:** A confirmed manufacturer backorder will not be shortened by reminders; communicate alternatives and expected dates instead.

For a new customer, request records corresponding to the evidence ledger. A matching story is a retrieval cue, not proof of the cause. Resolve contradictory dates, identities and authority before selecting an intervention.

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Assign one repair owner; require exact part verification before purchase; separate waiting for information, approval, supplier and customer.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** Track dependency states, deadline reminders and linked loan/return obligations; prepare customer updates from verified status.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.

AI may extract drafts, retrieve candidate cases and explain differences. Permissions, approvals, counters, calculations, state transitions and audit history belong in deterministic application code.
