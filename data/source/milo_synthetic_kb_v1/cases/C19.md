## Business and opening narrative

19 staff; service management software introduced eight months ago.

“The office creates tickets then messages technicians because chat gets noticed. Updates return by chat and are copied back. Long mobile forms and poor connectivity push reporting into the evening. Management asks for separate Excel totals because the dashboard is incomplete.”

## Current workflow and failure boundary

2. Office duplicates assignment in chat
3. Technician works

## Intervention comparison

1. **Process first:** Observe actual work; remove unjustified fields; define one authoritative queue and reconcile exceptions before retiring the shadow sheet.
2. **Conventional software and bounded AI assistance:** First configure existing software; add reliable notifications and offline draft synchronization only where supported and needed.
3. **Reject as the default:** wholesale platform replacement or autonomous agents without evidence that a simpler process/configuration change is inadequate.
