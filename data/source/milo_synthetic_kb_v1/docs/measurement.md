# Benefits without invented precision

Separate four outcomes: service quality, elapsed time, active staff time and cash impact. A faster response is not automatically a labor saving. A reclaimed hour is not automatically cash savings or removable headcount. Some capacity must remain available for urgent work and contractual coverage.

Collect matched baseline and pilot windows with stable event definitions. Include quiet and busy periods where relevant. Record volume, missing data, exceptions and additional review work. With a small non-random pilot, describe observed associations and limitations rather than claiming proven causal effects.

Task-time model:

`net_hours_per_period = eligible_events * (baseline_minutes - post_minutes) / 60 - extra_review_hours - maintenance_hours`

Inputs must have units, source, period and evidence status. If task stages overlap, use an end-to-end measurement or a non-overlapping task ledger; never add savings from intake, ticket creation and transcription when they are the same minutes. Negative savings are valid. Unknown inputs stay null, not zero. Use sensitivity ranges only with explicit assumptions.

Cash model, when justified:

`net_cash_benefit = evidenced_avoidable_cost + evidenced_incremental_margin - recurring_cost`

Avoid equating all salary cost with avoidable cost. Include implementation, migration, training and support costs in a separately reviewed payback calculation. No figures in these synthetic cases may be promoted to empirical inputs for a new customer.
